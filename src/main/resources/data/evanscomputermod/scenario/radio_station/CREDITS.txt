Music for the radio_station scenario
====================================

All five recordings come from Wikimedia Commons and are in the public domain
or dedicated to it (CC0 / Public Domain Mark), so they can be shipped and
broadcast freely. The files here were re-encoded for AM broadcast: mono,
8000 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV, band-limited to 80-3600 Hz, loudness-levelled
(ffmpeg dynaudnorm), cut to at most 3 minutes with a 4-second fade-out, and
tagged with a title (LIST/INFO INAM) that radio_station prints as
"now playing: ...".

01_wedding_march.wav
  Felix Mendelssohn - A Midsummer Night's Dream, Op. 61: Wedding March
  Performer: European Archive recording
  License: Public domain
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_Midsummer_Night%27s_Dream_Op._61_Wedding_March_(Mendelssohn)_European_Archive.ogg

02_jesu_joy.wav
  Johann Sebastian Bach - Cantata BWV 147, No. 10 "Jesus bleibet meine Freude"
  (Jesu, Joy of Man's Desiring)
  Performer: Orchestra Gli Armonici
  License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bach,_BWV_147,_10._Jesus_bleibet_meine_Freude.ogg

03_goldberg_aria.wav
  Johann Sebastian Bach - Goldberg Variations, BWV 988: Aria (Musopen version)
  Performer: as tagged in the file, Shelley Katz (Musopen Kickstarter Project, 2012)
  License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bach,_Goldberg_Variations,_Aria_(Musopen_version).ogg

04_scriabin_prelude.wav
  Alexander Scriabin - Prelude No. 1, Op. 67
  Performer: as tagged in the file, Jennifer Castellano (Musopen / pianosociety.com, 2002)
  License: Public domain
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alexander_Scriabin_-_prelude_no._1,_op._67.ogg

05_vivaldi_mandolin.wav
  Antonio Vivaldi - Mandolin Concerto in C major, RV 425
  Performer: The Milan Baroque Soloists
  License: Public Domain Mark (released into the public domain by the owner)
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Antonio_Vivaldi,_Mandolin_Concerto_in_C_major,_RV_425.ogg
