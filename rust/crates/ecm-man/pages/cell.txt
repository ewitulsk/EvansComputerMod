NAME
    cell - show and control this computer's cellular connection

SYNOPSIS
    cell [status]

    cell scan

    cell up | down

    cell roaming on|off

DESCRIPTION
    Talks to celld, the cellular daemon, and shows what it is doing:
    like the signal bars and carrier name on a phone, in detail. celld
    must be running (an ePhone starts it by itself once it has a modem
    and a SIM; on a Terminal run celld &).

COMMANDS
    status
        The default. The modem, the state with a sentence explaining
        it, the SIM (carrier and IMSI), the serving cell and channel,
        the signal (RSSI dBm, SINR dB, CQI, bars like [****]), the IP
        address with gateway and DNS, traffic and handovers, and
        whether roaming is on.

    scan
        The cells heard in the last 10 seconds: carrier, cell id,
        profile, channel, RSSI and SINR, the serving one marked. celld
        listens on one channel at a time while it searches, so an
        empty list may fill in a few seconds.

    up, down
        Attach to the network, or detach and switch the modem's radio
        off (airplane mode for the modem).

    roaming on|off
        Data roaming, saved in /etc/celld.toml.

STATES
    The states cell shows (each with a sentence explaining it):

      no_sim      no SIM card in the phone's tray or the modem's bay
      locked      the phone is locked to another carrier
      down        switched off (cell up to attach)
      searching   looking for a cell of the SIM's carrier
      waiting     waiting before trying again
      attaching   authenticating with the network
      connected   on a cell, with an IP address on wwan0
      idle        on a cell, quiet (resumes on traffic)
      handover    moving to a better cell (the address stays)
      detaching   leaving the network (after cell down)
      rejected    the network refused the phone (cell up to retry)

EXAMPLES
      cell
      cell scan
      cell down
      cell up

NOTES
    "celld is not running" means a modem is fitted but celld is not
    started. "this computer has no cellular modem": fit an LTE Modem or
    mmWave Modem in a module bay (right-click the computer with a
    Module Expansion Card, then the modem) and give it a SIM card.
