NAME
    discordd - a Discord backend: servers, text and voice channels

SYNOPSIS
    discordd [PORT] [--name NAME] [--data DIR]

DESCRIPTION
    Hosts Discord servers for the Discord app and the chat command: TCP
    PORT (default 7777) for both, UDP PORT+1 for voice. A backend hosts
    up to 32 servers. Its home server, named NAME (default Discord), is
    joined by every user on their first visit and owned by nobody; chat
    users talk in its first text channel, #general.

    Servers, channels, members and the last 50 messages of every text
    channel are kept in DIR/state (default /var/discord), written on
    every change. Voice frames are forwarded to everyone else in the
    channel.

    It announces itself to disco serve (as discord and chat), so run
    disco serve & on the same computer for apps to find it. Stop it with
    Ctrl+C; start it with & to keep the shell.

EXAMPLES
      discordd &
      discordd 7790 --name Village --data /var/village &
      disco serve &
