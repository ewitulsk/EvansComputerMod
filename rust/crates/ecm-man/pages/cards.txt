NAME
    cards - bank, NFC, hybrid and SIM cards: readers, printer, programs

DESCRIPTION
    Cards are items with chips and stripes that programs read and write
    through blocks next to a computer. Kinds: bank (a chip that signs
    payments, plus a stripe), NFC (sectors with keys, read over the air),
    hybrid (both) and SIM (for phones). Every card has two pixel-art
    faces that a Card Printer prints.

HARDWARE
    Card Reader/Writer
        A slot for one card (by hand or hopper). Right-click with a card
        to insert it, empty-handed to take it out. Auto Eject pushes a
        card out when a program is done. The card reader module is the
        same reader in a computer's bay.

    NFC Reader
        A pad that energises a near-field radio field and talks to cards
        held near it (an NFC module does the same from a bay, and follows
        a phone's holder).

    Card Printer
        Prints a design on a card, using one dye per colour. Hoppers feed
        blank cards and take printed ones.

PROGRAMS
    cardtool works every device from the shell. Designs (.card files)
    are written with the cards module and printed with cardtool print.

    The village App Store has an example app: bankd (a bank: writes
    cards, checks signatures, keeps balances).

SCRIPTING
    The cards module (import cards / require("cards")):
      cards.Reader.find()          the reader; reader.card()
      card.bank.sign(nonce, n)     a payment signature
      card.stripe.read()           the stripe tracks
      cards.Nfc.find().poll()      cards in the NFC field
      nfc.session(uid, sector, key)  read and write a sector
      cards.Printer.find().print(cards.Design.load(path))
      cards.Design                 load/save .card, set_pixel, fill, text
      cards.verify_tag(...)        check a signature (a bank's side)

FILES
    *.card
        A design: kind, palette, both faces and the printed fields, as
        JSON, written by cards.Design.save.
