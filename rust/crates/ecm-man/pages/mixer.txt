NAME
    mixer - the Volume Mixer: each app's volume, mute, speaker and meter

SYNOPSIS
    mixer
    mixer set <app|master[.side]> <percent>
    mixer mute <app|master[.side]> [on|off|toggle]
    mixer unmute <app|master[.side]>
    mixer route <app> <side|->
    mixer gain <microphone> <0-100>
    mixer reset <app>
    mixer watch

DESCRIPTION
    Every program that plays sound gets its own strip in the Volume
    Mixer, as on a real computer. Each Speaker (an ePhone's speaker,
    AirPods) has a master strip, and each Microphone an input strip.
    mixer with no arguments prints them all with a text level meter:

      SPEAKERS
        master.left    100%       ||||||||||||........  Speaker
      APPS
        dev.ecm.music   80%       ||||||||............  on left, ducked
        aplay          100% muted ....................  idle
      MICROPHONES
        top         gain 50       ||||||..............  -42 dB

    An app's volume goes from 0 to 150 percent (100 is as the app plays
    it); a speaker's from 0 to 100. The meter shows the loudest sound
    of the last moment on a -60 to 0 dB scale. "on left" is the speaker
    the app plays on now, "-> right" a speaker it was routed to, "idle"
    an app that is not playing. "ducked" means a call (a voice or phone
    stream) is lowering the app right now; it comes back by itself when
    the call goes quiet.

    The settings are kept per app on the computer, so an app keeps its
    volume the next time it plays. Settings > Mixer shows the same
    strips with sliders.

COMMANDS
    set <target> <percent>
        Set an app's volume (0-150) or a speaker's (master, or
        master.left for the speaker on the left side; 0-100). A
        trailing % is allowed.

    mute <target> [on|off|toggle]
        Silence an app or a speaker (on when nothing is given).

    unmute <target>
        Undo mute.

    route <app> <side>
        On a Terminal with several speakers, play the app on the
        speaker attached on that side. - (or default) plays it on the
        default speaker again (Settings > Sound).

    gain <microphone> <0-100>
        A microphone's input gain by its side (50 is the capsule's own
        level, 0 mutes it).

    reset <app>
        Forget an app's volume, mute and route.

    watch
        Live meters, redrawn twice a second. q, Esc or Ctrl+C quits.

    -h
        The list of commands.

NOTES
    Anyone may look at the mixer. Changing it from an app needs the
    mixer permission (the app lists it in its app.toml); programs typed
    in the shell may always change it. A refused change says why: no
    such speaker or microphone, a value out of range, or no permission.

    Python and Lua have the same commands: audio.mixer() (see
    scripting).

EXAMPLES
      mixer
      mixer set dev.ecm.music 60
      mixer set master 80
      mixer mute dev.ecm.discord
      mixer route aplay right
      mixer gain top 70
      mixer watch
