NAME
    fg - bring a background or stopped job to the foreground

SYNOPSIS
    fg [%job | pid]

DESCRIPTION
    Moves a job of this shell to the foreground: the shell waits for it
    to finish before showing the prompt again, your typing goes to its
    input, and Ctrl+C or Ctrl+T stops it (Ctrl+Z pauses it again). A
    stopped job (paused with Ctrl+Z) goes on running. Name the job by its
    number with a % in front (as jobs shows it), or by the PID of one of
    its processes; without an argument fg takes the newest job. It prints
    the job's command line.

    If there is no such job, fg prints "fg: no such job".

EXAMPLES
      chatd &
      [1] 5
      fg %1
      fg 5
      fg
