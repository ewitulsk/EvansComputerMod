NAME
    fg - bring a background job to the foreground

SYNOPSIS
    fg %job
    fg pid

DESCRIPTION
    Moves a background job of this shell to the foreground: the shell
    waits for it to finish before showing the prompt again, your typing
    goes to its input, and Ctrl+T stops it. Name the job by its job
    number with a % in front (as jobs shows it), or by the PID of one of
    its processes.

    If there is no such background job, fg prints "fg: no such job".
    Without an argument it prints its usage.

EXAMPLES
      chatd &
      [1] 5
      fg %1
      fg 5
