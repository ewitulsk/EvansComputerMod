NAME
    video - show, list and play video files

SYNOPSIS
    video info file
    video ls [dir ...]
    video play file [--screen] [--size WxH] [--start seconds] [--loop]
               [--mute]

DESCRIPTION
    Works with video files from the command line. The host decodes the
    video (FFmpeg), so the server needs the evanscomputermod-ffmpeg
    add-on; without it video says video isn't installed. The desktop
    has the same player as an app, Video Player (videoplayer).

COMMANDS
    info file
        The video's size, frame rate, number of frames, length and its
        sound (rate and mono or stereo, or none).

    ls [dir ...]
        The video files (.mp4 .m4v .mkv .webm .mov .avi) in the given
        directories, default /home and /home/videos, with their file
        size, length, picture size and whether they have sound.

    play file
        Plays the video on the terminal's graphics layer, with its sound
        on the computer's first speaker if there is one. It prints what
        it plays first and, at the end, how many frames it showed (and
        how many came late). Ctrl+T stops it.

OPTIONS
    --screen
        Play on the Screen wall attached to the computer, at the wall's
        size, instead of the terminal.

    --size WxH
        The box the picture is fitted into on the terminal, each side 2
        to 4096 (default 320x200).

    --start seconds
        Start that far into the video.

    --loop
        Start again from the beginning at the end, until Ctrl+T.

    --mute
        No sound.

NOTES
    video play shows each frame on the terminal as it is decoded, paced
    by its own clock. player and the Video Player app (videoplayer)
    queue frames ahead for their time instead, which stays smoother
    when the server lags; see gfx.

    File names are taken from the root of the computer's storage, not
    from the current directory: write /home/clip.mp4 even when you are
    in /home.

EXAMPLES
      video ls
      video info /home/videos/trailer.mp4
      video play /home/videos/trailer.mp4
      video play /home/clip.mp4 --screen --loop --mute
      video play /home/clip.mp4 --start 90 --size 160x90
