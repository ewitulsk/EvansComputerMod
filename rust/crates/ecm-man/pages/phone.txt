NAME
    phone - the ePhone's shell: home screen, status bar, app switching

SYNOPSIS
    phone [--restart]

DESCRIPTION
    phone (app id dev.ecm.phone) is the shell of every ePhone. The
    phone's GUI starts it when the phone boots; you don't type it. It
    shows one app at a time, between a status bar at the top and a
    navigation bar at the bottom. For how to use an ePhone in the game
    (holding it, hotkeys, SIM, battery, dock) see phone-guide.

    Home screen: the wallpaper and a grid of the installed apps (4
    columns, 3 on a Tier 1 panel), each with its icon or a coloured tile
    with its initial, and the shell's own Controls entry. Tap an app to
    open it.

    Status bar: the clock, a dot for unread notifications, an orange MIC
    pill while an app you can't see is listening to the microphone, a
    pill for each background mode in use (CALL, AUDIO, RADIO, DL, NAV),
    the carrier and cellular bars, Wi-Fi bars, and the battery
    percentage. Tap it (or pull it down) for the notification sheet.

    Notification sheet, top to bottom:

    Now Playing: a card for each app that is playing something (newest
    first) with its cover art, title, artist and progress, and Previous,
    Play or Pause, and Next buttons that control that app. The card goes
    away when the app stops its session.

    Volume: the speaker icon (tap to mute or unmute) and a slider for the
    phone speaker's master volume. The arrow at the right expands it into
    a strip per app that makes sound: its name, a slider from 0 to 150 %
    (the mark is 100 %), Mute, a level meter, and a "ducked" badge while
    a call or voice chat lowers it; then the microphone's gain with its
    level. While the sheet is open the strips are read about twice a
    second. Drag or tap a slider to set it. Settings has the same Volume
    Mixer.

    Notifications: tap one to open its app, or Clear all.

    Navigation bar: Back, Home and Recent apps. Recent apps shows the
    open apps; tap one to show it, X to close it.

    Controls lists the hotkey slots each open app has claimed (the app
    on screen first) and the keys below.

    When an app asks for a permission, a dialog offers Allow, Only this
    time and Don't allow. The first time an app wants to keep working
    after you leave it (a call, music, radio, a download, navigation),
    the dialog asks "Keep <app> running in the background for calls?"
    (or audio, radio, downloads, navigation) with Allow and Don't allow;
    the answer is remembered (change it in Settings > Apps).

    An app that leaves the screen is suspended: it saves its state and
    stops until it is shown again (see phone-guide for the exceptions).

OPTIONS
    --restart
        Given when the shell is restarted after a crash: the apps still
        open are kept and the last session is not reopened again.

KEYS
    These work while the phone is open (right-click with it in hand).

    Alt+H
        Home screen.

    Alt+Backspace
        Back: closes the sheet or dialog shown, otherwise goes home.

    Alt+Tab
        Show the next open app.

    Alt+N
        The notification sheet.

    Plain Backspace and Home go to the app (text fields need them). Esc
    closes the phone screen.

FILES
    /dev/mixer
        The Volume Mixer the sheet's slider and strips read and change
        (set master, set <app>, mute <app> toggle, gain <mic>).
