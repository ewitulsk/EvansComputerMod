NAME
    traceroute - show the routers on the way to a computer

SYNOPSIS
    traceroute <host> [max-hops]

DESCRIPTION
    Sends ICMP echo probes with a time-to-live of 1, 2, 3, ... Each
    router that drops a probe because its TTL ran out answers, so every
    line shows the hop number and the address of one router on the
    path. A hop that does not answer within one second prints *. It
    stops when the target itself answers (or reports it unreachable),
    or after max-hops hops (default 30).

    Addresses are printed as numbers; there are no reverse names yet.

EXAMPLES
      traceroute 100.72.0.10
      traceroute web.v8.ring 12
