NAME
    desktop - the GUI's desktop shell: taskbar, windows and launcher

SYNOPSIS
    desktop [--restart] [--launch PROGRAM[:ARG:ARG...]]...

DESCRIPTION
    desktop (app id dev.ecm.desktop) is the shell the GUI starts by
    default; you don't type it, gui starts it. It draws the wallpaper, a
    taskbar at the bottom of the screen, and a title bar and border
    around every app window.

    The taskbar holds the Apps button (the launcher), one button per
    open window (click to focus it, click the focused one to minimize
    it), the tray, Wi-Fi bars (a click opens Settings on Wi-Fi),
    cellular bars when a modem is fitted, and the clock (HH:MM).

    The tray shows an icon for each app that is working in the
    background (in a call, playing audio, a radio, a download,
    navigation; a dot marks it) and for each app whose window you hid
    there. Closing the window of such an app (the close button, Close
    in the title bar's menu, or Alt+F4) hides it to the tray instead of
    quitting it, so the call or the music goes on. Other apps close as
    usual. Click a tray icon to show its window again; right-click it
    for Open and Quit (Quit closes its windows, and the app ends).

    The speaker icon in the tray (when the computer has a speaker)
    opens the Volume Mixer: Now Playing for each app that plays
    something (title, artist, progress, and Previous, Play or Pause,
    Next), a master slider and Mute for each speaker, a strip per app
    (a slider from 0 to 150 %, Mute, a level meter, a "ducked" badge
    while a call lowers it, and with several speakers a button that
    picks which speaker it plays on), and the microphone's gain with its
    level. It is read twice a second while open; click the speaker
    again or outside it to close it.

    The launcher's Console item goes back to the console (the GUI keeps
    running; gui show or Ctrl+Alt+Backspace returns). The launcher lists
    the core apps (Terminal, Files, Text Editor,
    Settings, Notes, Visual Programmer), then every app installed under
    /apps, the App Store among them. It is read again each time it
    opens, so an app you just installed is there at once.

    Windows: drag the title bar to move a window; drag its border or a
    corner to resize it. An outline follows the pointer and the window
    is placed when you let go. The title bar has minimize, maximize and
    close buttons. Double-click the title to maximize; right-click it
    for a menu (Minimize, Maximize or Restore, Close). On a small
    (compact) display every window opens maximized; new windows
    cascade. A minimized app keeps running.

    Notifications from apps show as cards in the top right corner for 6
    seconds; clicking one (or one of its actions) passes it to the app.
    When an app asks for a permission, a dialog asks "Allow <app> to use
    <permission>?", says what the permission covers, and offers
    "Remember my choice", Deny and Allow. The first time an app wants to
    keep working in the background it asks "Keep <app> running in the
    background for calls?" (or audio, radio, downloads, navigation)
    with Don't allow and Allow; that answer is always remembered.

    At its first start the desktop opens again the apps that were
    running when the computer last shut down (not after a restart).

OPTIONS
    --launch PROGRAM[:ARG:ARG...]
        Start PROGRAM (or an installed app id) when the desktop starts,
        with its arguments separated by colons. May be repeated. Give it
        in /etc/ui.toml as shell_args.

    --restart
        Given by the GUI when it restarts the shell after a crash: the
        open windows get their title bars back and neither the --launch
        programs nor the last session's apps are started a second time.

KEYS
    Alt+Tab
        The window switcher, most recently used first. Each press moves
        on; the choice is focused a moment after the last press.

    Alt+F4
        Close the focused window (hide it to the tray if its app is
        working in the background).

    Ctrl+Esc
        Open the launcher.

FILES
    /etc/ui.toml
        theme is sent to every app at start and again whenever the file
        changes (checked every second), so Settings > Appearance
        restyles every open window live. shell_args are the desktop's
        arguments, for example:

      shell_args = ["--launch", "terminal", "--launch", "files:/home"]

    /dev/mixer
        The Volume Mixer the tray's popup reads and changes.
