NAME
    jobs - list background jobs

SYNOPSIS
    jobs

DESCRIPTION
    Lists the jobs this shell started in the background (with & at the
    end of the line) that are still running, as

      [1]  Running    httpd 8080 &

    The number in brackets is the job number that fg %N takes. With no
    background jobs it prints "No background jobs." When a background
    job ends, the shell prints "[N]+ Done    command" before the next
    prompt.

    Each shell has its own jobs: jobs started over SSH or in another
    Terminal window are not listed here (ps shows every process).
