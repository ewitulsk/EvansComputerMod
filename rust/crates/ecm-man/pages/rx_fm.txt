NAME
    rx_fm - listen to FM radio through a Speaker

SYNOPSIS
    rx_fm <freq> [--wide] [--squelch DB] [--gain DB] [--sdr NAME]
    [--rate SPS] [--speaker SIDE] [--volume 0-100] [--seconds S]
    [--wav FILE [--no-speaker]] [--audio-rate HZ]

DESCRIPTION
    Tunes the SDR next to the computer to freq, demodulates narrowband
    FM (voice, VHF ham channels, the Handheld Radio's VHF band) and
    plays the audio on a Speaker touching the computer. With --wide it
    receives broadcast FM (88-108 MHz) instead.

    It prints one line saying what it receives, then every 2 seconds the
    signal level in dBFS, and runs until Ctrl+T (or --seconds). With
    --seconds it ends with a summary: the strongest audio tone heard, or
    "no audio tone (noise)".

    Frequencies are written 146.52M, 146.52MHz, 146520k or 146.52e6.

OPTIONS
    --wide
        Broadcast (wide) FM: 75 kHz deviation, de-emphasis, 15 kHz
        audio. Needs a sample rate of at least 150000 (default 240000,
        so a Standard or Advanced SDR).

    --squelch DB
        Mute the audio while the signal is below DB (dBFS, e.g. -40).
        Off by default.

    --gain DB
        Fixed receiver gain in dB. Default: the SDR's automatic gain
        (AGC).

    --sdr NAME
        Which SDR: empty (the first), an index (0, sdr0, sdr_0), a side
        of the computer (left, top, ...) or a /dev/sdr path.

    --rate SPS
        Sample rate. Default 48000 (240000 with --wide).

    --speaker SIDE, --volume 0-100
        Which Speaker to use and its volume.

    --seconds S
        Stop after S seconds of samples.

    --wav FILE
        Also write the audio to a WAV file (complete on disk even when
        stopped with Ctrl+T). With --no-speaker, only to the file: no
        Speaker is needed.

    --audio-rate HZ
        Audio sample rate, 8000-48000. Default 24000 (48000 wide).

EXAMPLES
      rx_fm 146.52M
      rx_fm 98.1M --wide --volume 60
      rx_fm 146.52M --squelch -45 --seconds 30 --wav /voice.wav

NOTES
    Errors: "can't open the first SDR (...); place an SDR block next to
    the computer", and "no speaker (...); place a Speaker next to the
    computer or use --wav FILE --no-speaker". --help prints the usage.
