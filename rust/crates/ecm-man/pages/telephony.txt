NAME
    telephony - phone numbers, calls, texts and picture messages

DESCRIPTION
    A SIM written by a carrier's core (cored) has a phone number. A
    phone with that SIM in its modem attaches to the carrier's cells,
    gets a second address for calls (the IMS bearer, ims0) and registers
    with the carrier's IMS server (imsd). Then it can:

      - call any number: VoLTE, with HD voice when both ends have it
      - send texts (SMS) and pictures, voice notes, contact cards and
        group texts (MMS)
      - get voicemail when it doesn't answer, and listen in the Phone app
      - reach numbers on other carriers that peer with its carrier

    The status bar shows VoLTE once the phone is registered. Calls keep
    going in a pocket or the hotbar; an incoming call wakes a sleeping
    phone (the cell pages it) and rings.

APPS
    Phone (dev.ecm.dialer)
        Favourites, Recents, Contacts, Keypad and Voicemail; the
        incoming-call screen (Answer, Decline, Message) and the in-call
        screen (mute, keypad, speaker, hold, add call, swap, merge, end).
        See dialer.

    Messages (dev.ecm.messages)
        Conversations, threads with Sent / Delivered / Failed, groups,
        pictures, voice notes and contact cards. See messages.

    Contacts (dev.ecm.contacts)
        The address book (/home/.contacts/contacts.vcf), My card, vCard
        share and import. See contacts.

NUMBERS
    Numbers look like +1 214 555 0142 (E.164). Each carrier has blocks
    of numbers (a Tech Village has its own area code); cored gives a new
    SIM the next free one. Dial a local number without the +1 and the
    area code, or a short code:

      611        the carrier's support line (1: balance, 2: your number)
      *86        voicemail
      *67 <n>    withhold your number for this call
      *72 <n>    forward all calls to n; *73 stops

    Forwarding when busy, unanswered or unreachable, call waiting, Do
    Not Disturb and a subscriber's blocked numbers are set by the
    carrier (cored feature).

    Numbers move between carriers with a port code (cored port-code).

SCRIPTS
    Python and Lua have a phone module with the same names:

      import phone
      print(phone.number())
      phone.sms_send("+12145550143", "On my way")
      c = phone.dial("611"); c.send_tones("1"); c.hangup()
      phone.on_sms(lambda sender, text: print(sender, text))
      phone.run()

    number() status() sms_send(to, text) sms_status(id) sms_inbox()
    threads() messages(thread) mark_read(thread)
    mms_send(to_list, text, files)  dial(number, hide) calls()
    speaker(on) call_log()  on_sms(fn) on_mms(fn) on_call(fn) poll(s)
    run()  contacts() add_contact(name, number, label)
    A Call: id number state() info() wait() answer() reject() hangup()
    hold(on) mute(on) send_tones(digits) tones() play(wav)
    record(seconds, path).

    Errors raise phone.PhoneError (Lua: an error starting "phone: ").
    Files a script hands to telephonyd (play, record, MMS parts) must be
    under /home/ or /tmp/.

PERMISSIONS
    phone       calls and everything else (telephonyd's port 7720)
    sms         texts only (port 7726)
    contacts    the address book
    The built-in apps have them. Another app asks once, and each call or
    text it starts is confirmed by the player.
