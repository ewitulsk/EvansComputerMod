NAME
    sms - send and read text and picture messages from the shell

SYNOPSIS
    sms send <number> <text...>

    sms mms <number[,number...]> [--text T] [--file F]...

    sms [list]

    sms read <number|thread>

    sms delete <id>

    sms watch

DESCRIPTION
    Talks to telephonyd, the phone's messaging daemon, on 127.0.0.1
    TCP 7720: the Messages app as commands. telephonyd must be running
    (an ePhone starts it; on a Terminal with a modem run telephonyd &)
    and registered with the carrier.

COMMANDS
    send <number> <text...>
        Send a text; the rest of the line is the message. A long text
        goes as several parts (sms says how many) and arrives as one.
        Prints the message's id.

    mms <number[,number...]> [--text T] [--file F]...
        Send a picture message: files (.png pictures, .wav voice
        notes, .vcf contact cards; under /home/ or /tmp/) and text.
        Words that are not options are added to the text. Several
        numbers, separated by commas, make a group: everyone gets the
        whole list, so replies go to the whole group.

    list
        The default. The conversations, newest first: a * when there
        are unread messages, the other numbers (group when there are
        several) and the start of the last message.

    read <number|thread>
        A conversation, oldest first, and mark it read. Your messages
        show their status (sending, sent, delivered, failed: why) and
        a picture message its files. For a group give the numbers in
        E.164 form, separated by commas, as the thread key.

    delete <id>
        Delete one message.

    watch
        Print messages as they arrive, and sent, delivered and failed
        notices for yours. Ctrl+C stops.

FILES
    /home/Messages/
        Pictures and other parts of the messages received.

EXAMPLES
      sms send +12145550143 On my way
      sms send 22225 BAL
      sms list
      sms read 214-555-0143
      sms mms +12145550143 --file /home/Pictures/house.png Look
      sms mms +12145550143,+12145550144 Party at 8

NOTES
    Texts to a 5 or 6 digit short code go to a program at the carrier
    (see smscd); 22225 BAL answers with your data balance.
