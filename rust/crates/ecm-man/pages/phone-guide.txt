NAME
    phone-guide - using the ePhone: states, hotkeys, SIM, battery, apps

DESCRIPTION
    The ePhone is a computer you carry. It is crafted from 6 iron, a
    glass pane, a Color LCD panel and a Wi-Fi Module, and comes with the
    panel and the Wi-Fi Module fitted and a full battery. It always runs
    the GUI: a home screen of apps (see phone).

HOLDING IT
    Main hand or off hand
        Awake: it runs and its screen shows in your hand. The first time
        it boots.

    In the hotbar, not selected
        Asleep: everything is paused, but its modules stay on (Wi-Fi
        stays connected). Apps may get short background wakes.

    Anywhere else (inventory, chest, the ground, the dock)
        Off: paused, radios off the air. Picked up again it carries on
        where it was.

    Logging out, an empty battery or a server restart stop the phone:
    it boots again next time, and the apps that were open come back in
    the background with the state they saved.

USING IT
    Right-click
        Open it full screen. Every key and the mouse go to the phone;
        the mouse is your finger. Esc closes it.

    Sneak + right-click
        The slot screen: the display panel (Tier 1 to 3), two module
        slots, the SIM tray, and the toggles Share screen and Others'
        phones.

HOTKEYS
    Controls > ePhone has ePhone slot 1 to 6 and ePhone: switch app,
    all unbound at first. They work while a phone is in your main or
    off hand and no screen is open, so you can use the phone while you
    walk. A slot goes to the app on screen, if it claimed it (Notes:
    slot 1 adds a note; GBA: slots 1-6 are A, B, Left, Right, Up,
    Start; Video Player: play/pause, back and forward 10 s). switch app
    shows the next open app. The phone's Controls entry lists the
    slots each app claimed. A key used for walking still walks.

    With the phone open: Alt+H home, Alt+Backspace back, Alt+Tab next
    app, Alt+N notifications.

SIM AND CELLULAR
    Put a SIM card in the SIM tray and an LTE or mmWave modem in a
    module slot: the phone joins a carrier's cell network and shows its
    bars and carrier name in the status bar. Taking the SIM out drops
    the link at once. Settings > Cellular shows the details.

BATTERY AND DOCK
    The battery drains while the phone is awake (more with a bigger
    panel), slowly while asleep, and more for each radio module in
    use. At 0 % the phone turns off and stays off until charged. The
    item shows a charge bar below 100 %.

    The Charging Dock (2 gold nuggets, redstone, 3 iron): right-click
    it with the phone to dock it, with an empty hand to take it back.
    Its lamp is lit while it charges. It also takes FE from any side;
    a server may make charging need FE. Server owners set the rates in
    serverconfig/evanscomputermod-phone-server.toml.

APPS
    Built in: Terminal (a shell, 30 columns wide), Notes, GBA, Settings,
    Files (your documents, /home), the App Store, the Text Editor, and
    the Video Player where the server has it. Install more from the App
    Store or with pkg in the Terminal app.

    One app is on screen at a time. An app you leave is suspended: it
    saves its state and stops. Exceptions, which you allow per app in
    Settings > Apps or with pkg perm: background refresh (a short wake
    every few minutes, e.g. Chat fetching messages) and background
    audio (music keeps playing).

    A program can read the phone's battery: peripheral.find("ephone")
    gives battery(), state() and info().

OTHERS SEEING IT
    Players near you may see your screen, a plain glow or nothing,
    depending on the server, your Share screen toggle and their Others'
    phones toggle (both saved in
    config/evanscomputermod-phone-client.toml). Only you can type on
    your phone.

WIRELESS CONTROLLER
    Hold the phone in one hand and right-click with a Wireless Xbox
    Controller in the other: it pairs with the phone, and apps see it as
    controller_1 to controller_4.
