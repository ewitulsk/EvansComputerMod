NAME
    man - read the manual page of a command or topic

SYNOPSIS
    man <name>

    man -k <word>

    man -l

    man -P <name>

DESCRIPTION
    Shows the manual page for a command (man ls) or a topic (man keys,
    man networking) in a pager: Space for the next page, b to go back,
    /text to find, q to quit, h for all the pager's keys. Paragraphs are
    re-wrapped to the screen, so pages read well on an ePhone too. A page
    that fits on the screen is just printed.

    help lists every page by subject; man intro is a good start.

OPTIONS
    -k word
        Search: list the pages whose name or one-line summary contains
        word (like apropos).

    -l
        List every page with its summary.

    -P
        Print the page without the pager (for > file or | grep).

FILES
    /usr/share/man/<name>.txt
        A page of your own, or a replacement for a built-in one. The
        format is plain text: see the built-in pages (man -P ls) for the
        layout. Headings are lines in capitals at the left edge; lines
        indented 4 or 8 spaces are paragraphs; other lines are shown as
        written.

EXAMPLES
      man ls
      man -k wifi
      man -P ping > /home/ping.txt

SEE ALSO
