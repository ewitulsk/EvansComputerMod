NAME
    beep - play a tone on a speaker

SYNOPSIS
    beep [hz] [ms] [--wave type] [--volume n] [--speaker side]

DESCRIPTION
    Plays one tone on a Speaker next to the computer: hz is the pitch
    (20 to 20000, default 440) and ms the length in milliseconds (1 to
    60000, default 200). The tone fades in and out over 5 ms so it does
    not click. Without a speaker, beep says
    "place a Speaker next to the computer." and exits with code 1.

OPTIONS
    --wave type
        The sound of the tone: square (the default), sine, triangle or
        saw.

    --volume n
        The speaker's volume, 0 to 100.

    --speaker side
        Which speaker to use when there are several, by the side it is
        attached on (left, right, ..., or a module bay such as
        left_bay_1). Default: the first speaker.

EXAMPLES
      beep
      beep 880 500
      beep 262 1000 --wave sine --volume 50
