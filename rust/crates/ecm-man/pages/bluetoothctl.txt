NAME
    bluetoothctl - the Bluetooth radio from the console

SYNOPSIS
    bluetoothctl [show]

    bluetoothctl devices | scan [SECONDS]

    bluetoothctl pair DEVICE | connect DEVICE | remove DEVICE

    bluetoothctl disconnect | power on|off

DESCRIPTION
    Works the computer's bluetooth peripheral (an ePhone has one), as
    Settings > Bluetooth does. DEVICE is an address or part of a name.

COMMANDS
    show
        The radio: its name and address, what is connected and where
        the sound goes.

    devices
        The paired devices.

    scan [SECONDS]
        Devices nearby (default 5 s): open an AirPods case's lid to see
        them.

    pair DEVICE
        Pair (then it connects).

    connect DEVICE, disconnect
        Connect a paired device; disconnect it (it stays paired).

    remove DEVICE
        Forget a paired device.

    power on, power off
        Switch the radio.

EXAMPLES
      bluetoothctl scan
      bluetoothctl pair AirPods
      bluetoothctl
