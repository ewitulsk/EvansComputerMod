NAME
    dialer - the Phone app: make and take calls on an ePhone

SYNOPSIS
    dialer
    dialer --number <number>
    dialer --call <number>
    dialer --incoming <call id>

DESCRIPTION
    Opens Phone (dev.ecm.dialer). It talks to telephonyd, the phone's
    calling service, on its control socket (127.0.0.1:7720), so it needs
    the phone permission, and it reads the address book that Contacts
    keeps in /home/.contacts/contacts.vcf. It needs the GUI (start it
    with gui); in the console use tel instead.

    The header shows the phone's own number and its VoLTE registration:
    VoLTE (registered, calls work), Registering, Registration failed or
    No service. When telephonyd does not answer, a card says so.

    Favourites: the contacts marked as favourites in Contacts. Tap one
    to call its first number.

    Recents: the call log, newest first, with the name (or the number),
    Incoming, Outgoing or Missed, how long the call lasted and how long
    ago it was. Missed calls are red and the tab shows how many there
    are. Tap a call to call back.

    Contacts: search by name or number; tap a contact to see its
    numbers and tap a number to call it.

    Keypad: the digits, * and #. The number is formatted as it is typed
    and the contact it belongs to is shown under it. Delete takes off
    the last digit; Call dials it. Call with nothing typed fills in the
    last number called. The keyboard works too: digits, Shift+3 for #,
    Shift+8 for *, Backspace and Enter.

    Voicemail: visual voicemail from the carrier, new messages marked
    and counted on the tab. Choose one, then Play (it is saved as
    /tmp/vm-<id>.wav, opened with its default app and marked heard),
    Mark heard, Call back or Delete.

    A call coming in fills the screen with the caller's name and number
    (No Caller ID when the number is hidden) and Answer, Decline and
    Message (declines and opens Messages to the caller). Hotkey 1
    answers and hotkey 2 declines. With a call already up (call
    waiting) the choices are End & answer, Hold & answer and Decline.

    During a call the screen shows the name and number, the time, HD for
    a wideband call, Muted, and the codec and packet loss. Buttons: Mute,
    Keypad (sends tones, for menus that say "press 1"), Speaker, Add
    call (the keypad over the call; the new call puts the first on
    hold), Hold or Resume, and with two calls Swap and Merge (a
    conference), then End. When the last call ends the tab that was
    open comes back.

    Tapping a missed call notification opens Recents; a voicemail
    notification opens Voicemail.

OPTIONS
    --number <number>
        Open the keypad with this number typed in.
    --call <number>
        Call this number straight away.
    --incoming <call id>
        Show the incoming call screen for this call. telephonyd starts
        Phone like this when a call rings in.

EXAMPLES
      dialer --call 611
      dialer --number 2145550142
