NAME
    bluetooth - the ePhone's Bluetooth radio and AirPods

DESCRIPTION
    Every ePhone has a Bluetooth radio. AirPods pair with it over real
    2.4 GHz radio frames, so range, walls and other 2.4 GHz traffic
    (Wi-Fi, controllers) matter. While connected AirPods are in your
    ears, the phone's sound plays only in them and its microphone is
    theirs.

ITEMS
    AirPods
        Wireless earbuds with microphones. Right-click to put them in
        your ears (the head slot). About three hours of battery while
        connected.

    AirPods Case
        Right-click opens or closes the lid (it closes by itself after
        a minute); the AirPods inside can be paired while it is open.
        Sneak + right-click takes them out and into your ears.

PAIRING
    Open the case's lid near your phone. On the phone, Settings >
    Bluetooth lists "<you>'s AirPods" as ready to pair: tap Pair. Put
    them in your ears: Settings says Sound plays on them. From then on
    worn AirPods connect by themselves; back in the case with the lid
    closed they disconnect and the sound goes back to the speaker.
    Disconnect keeps them paired, Forget unpairs them.

WHILE THEY ARE WORN
    Everything the phone plays (Discord, the GBA, beep, aplay) plays in
    your ears only: nobody else hears it and no microphone picks it up.
    /dev/mic hears your mouth through them, and Discord shows
    Headphones and talks full duplex.
