NAME
    wc - count lines, words and bytes

SYNOPSIS
    wc

DESCRIPTION
    Reads all of its input and prints three numbers: the number of
    lines, of words (runs of non-space characters) and of bytes. wc
    takes no options and reads no files named on the command line: feed
    it with a pipe or a < redirect.

EXAMPLES
      cat notes.txt | wc
      wc < notes.txt
      ls bin | wc

    cat, grep, shell
