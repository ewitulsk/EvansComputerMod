NAME
    history - list the commands you typed

SYNOPSIS
    history [N]

    history -c

DESCRIPTION
    Lists the lines entered at the shell, oldest first, with their
    numbers (for !N). The history holds the last 500 lines, is saved in
    /.history and is shared by the Terminal block, Terminal windows and
    SSH logins, so it survives a reboot.

OPTIONS
    N
        Only the last N lines.

    -c
        Forget the whole history.

EXAMPLES
      history
      history 10
      !12
