NAME
    videoplayer - the Video Player app for the desktop and the ePhone

SYNOPSIS
    videoplayer [file]

DESCRIPTION
    Opens the Video Player app in a window. It needs the GUI: start the
    desktop with gui first (on the console, use video play instead).
    Like video, it needs the server's FFmpeg add-on.

    Without a file it opens the library: the videos in /home and
    /home/videos (.mp4 .m4v .mkv .webm .mov .avi). Click one, or press
    Enter for the first one shown, to play it. With a file it plays that
    file (a name without a leading / still starts at the root, not the
    current directory). Files also opens videos with it.

    The window shows the picture with a seek bar and buttons under it;
    the sound goes to the computer's speaker. Leaving the app pauses it,
    and it remembers the file and position for next time.

KEYS
    Space or K
        Play or pause.

    Left, Right
        Back or forward 10 seconds.

    L
        Loop on or off.

    Esc or Backspace
        Back to the library.

    Hotkeys 1, 2, 3
        On an ePhone held in hand: play/pause, back 10 s, forward 10 s.

EXAMPLES
      videoplayer
      videoplayer /home/videos/trailer.mp4
