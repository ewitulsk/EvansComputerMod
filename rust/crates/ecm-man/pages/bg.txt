NAME
    bg - continue a job in the background

SYNOPSIS
    bg

DESCRIPTION
    Exists for people used to other shells. Programs are never paused in
    Terminal OS, so a background job is always already running; bg only
    prints "Background jobs already run independently."

    To start a program in the background, put & at the end of its
    command line.

EXAMPLES
      httpd 8080 &

SEE ALSO
    jobs, fg, shell
