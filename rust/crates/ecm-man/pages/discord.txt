NAME
    discord - Discord: servers, text channels and voice channels

SYNOPSIS
    discord

    discord --source <dir>

DESCRIPTION
    Opens the Discord app (dev.ecm.discord; it needs the GUI). It finds
    every Discord backend (discordd) on the network by itself, plus the
    one in /etc/chat.conf and the ones you added, and shows the servers
    you joined on all of them together on the rail at the left.

    The + button creates a server (Create My Own), joins one (Join a
    Server lists every server on every connected backend), searches the
    network, or adds a backend by address (10.0.8.1 or 10.0.8.1:7790).
    In a server, text channels show as # name and voice channels with a
    speaker icon and who is in them. Its owner adds channels and can
    delete it; others can leave it.

    In a text channel the last 50 messages show, and Enter sends.
    /nick NAME changes your nickname on every backend. A message while
    the window isn't on screen posts a notification.

    Tap a voice channel to join it: the app uses the default microphone
    and speaker (on an ePhone its own, or its AirPods, shown as
    Headphones). Mute and Deafen sit by your name; Disconnect leaves. On
    a loudspeaker it talks half duplex, like a speakerphone.

    On the ePhone the hotkeys are 1 Send, 2 Mute, 3 Hang up.

OPTIONS
    --source dir
        Write the app's package folder, for pkg build dir repo.

FILES
    /etc/chat.conf
        A backend to connect to (village computers have one) and the
        default nickname.

SEE ALSO
