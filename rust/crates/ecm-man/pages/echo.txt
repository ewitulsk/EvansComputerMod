NAME
    echo - print text

SYNOPSIS
    echo [-n] [-e] [text ...]

DESCRIPTION
    Prints its arguments separated by single spaces, followed by a new
    line. Together with > and >> it is the quickest way to write a line
    into a file.

OPTIONS
    -n
        Do not print the new line at the end.

    -e
        Turn backslash sequences into characters: \n new line, \t tab,
        \r carriage return, \\ a backslash, \0 a zero byte. -ne and -en
        combine both options.

EXAMPLES
      echo Hello world
      echo "nameserver 10.0.0.1" > /etc/resolv.conf
      echo -e "line one\nline two" >> notes.txt
