NAME
    music - the Music app: songs streamed from the music servers around

SYNOPSIS
    music
    music --source DIR

DESCRIPTION
    Opens the Music window (it needs the GUI; on an ePhone it is the
    Music app). Music finds the music servers (musicd) on the network by
    itself when it opens and every minute, and lists them; a server it
    can't find can be added by address in Settings. Each server shows
    your name when you are signed in, or Sign in.

    Home shows what you played recently, your playlists and what is new
    on the server. Search shows results as you type, grouped: the top
    result, songs, artists, albums and playlists. Library has your
    playlists, Liked Songs, albums and artists. Now Playing has the
    cover, a seek bar, play/pause, previous/next, shuffle, repeat and
    the queue.

    Tapping a song plays it and queues the list it is in. Next plays a
    song after this one, Queue adds it at the end, + adds it to a
    playlist. Your playlists can be renamed, reordered, made public or
    private, and deleted.

    A song starts once 3 seconds of it are in, and Music keeps 30 seconds
    ahead. When the music runs dry it shows Buffering... and goes on from
    the same moment; a dropped connection is picked up where it stopped.
    Settings > Streaming quality chooses Original (the MP3), Data saver
    (22 kHz mono, about 11 KB/s) or Auto (Data saver on cellular). A
    change applies from the next song.

    Music keeps playing in the background and in the pocket. The Now
    Playing card and the hotkeys control it: 1 play/pause, 2 previous,
    3 next. After the phone was off it opens on the same song, paused.

    The first sign-in to a server's accounts shows their key; Music
    remembers it and refuses a server with the same name or address and
    another key. Settings > Accounts lists the remembered keys; Forget
    removes one by hand.

OPTIONS
    --source DIR
        Write the app's package folder (app.toml, icon.png, main.wasm)
        into DIR, for pkg build DIR REPO.

FILES
    /var/apps/dev.ecm.music/settings
        The servers you added and the streaming quality.
    /var/apps/dev.ecm.music/accounts
        Remembered server keys and saved sign-ins.

EXAMPLES
      music
      music --source /tmp/music && pkg build /tmp/music /srv/repo

SEE ALSO
