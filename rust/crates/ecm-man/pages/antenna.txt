NAME
    antenna - measure the antenna on a feed point next to the computer

SYNOPSIS
    antenna [summary]

    antenna swr <f1> <f2> [points]

    antenna z <f>

    antenna polar <f> [az|el] [--step D]

    antenna limits [--amp W]

DESCRIPTION
    Reads the antenna built from wire around a Feed Point that touches
    the computer (peripheral type antenna), as the Antenna Analyzer
    does: where it resonates, how well it matches 50 ohms, its gain
    pattern and how much power it can take. The server solves the wire
    structure in the background; while it runs the program prints
    "antenna: solving..." and waits up to --wait seconds, then shows
    the estimate.

    Frequencies are written 7.1M, 7100k, 7.1e6 or 146.52MHz.

COMMANDS
    summary
        The default. Resonant frequency, 2:1 SWR band and power
        ratings, then the feed point's position and whether the result
        is solved or an estimate.

    swr <f1> <f2> [points]
        A text plot of SWR across f1..f2 (points 2-401, default 41),
        then the lowest SWR, the resonance and the 2:1 band.

    z <f>
        Impedance (R + jX ohms), SWR and efficiency at f.

    polar <f> [az|el]
        A polar plot of the gain pattern at f: az is the horizontal cut
        (north up) through the peak's elevation, el the vertical cut
        through the peak's bearing. Then the peak gain in dBi, its
        bearing and elevation, and the polarization.

    limits
        The power the antenna can take, its weakest part (wire or
        insulation), and whether the transmitter found on the
        feedline is within that rating.

OPTIONS
    --step D
        polar: angle step, 1-90 degrees. Default 5.

    --amp W
        limits: check a transmitter of W watts.

    --name P
        The antenna peripheral to read. Default the first one.

    --wait S
        How long to wait for the solver. Default 20.

    --gfx, --screen
        Also draw the plot on the terminal's display (--gfx) or the
        attached Screen (--screen), for --seconds S (default 15).

EXAMPLES
      antenna
      antenna swr 6.8M 7.6M
      antenna z 7.1M
      antenna polar 14.2M el --gfx
      antenna limits --amp 100

NOTES
    A dipole for f MHz has two arms of about 72 / f blocks each (40 m
    band: 10 blocks per arm, resonant near 7.2 MHz). "No antenna"
    errors mean the computer is not touching a Feed Point, or the feed
    point has no wire to measure.
