NAME
    sleep - wait for a number of milliseconds

SYNOPSIS
    sleep milliseconds

DESCRIPTION
    Does nothing for the given time, then exits. The time is in
    milliseconds (1000 is one second), not seconds as on other systems.
    Useful in scripts, or to test background jobs.

EXAMPLES
      sleep 1000
      sleep 60000 &

SEE ALSO
    jobs, shell
