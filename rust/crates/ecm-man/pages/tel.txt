NAME
    tel - make and control phone calls from the shell

SYNOPSIS
    tel [status]

    tel dial <number> [--hide]

    tel answer [id] | hangup [id] | decline [id]

    tel hold [id] on|off | mute [id] on|off | swap | merge

    tel speaker on|off

    tel dtmf <digits> [id]

    tel play <wav> [id] | record <seconds> <wav> [id]

    tel calls | log [n] | voicemail

    tel forward <number> | forward off

    tel watch

DESCRIPTION
    Talks to telephonyd, the phone's calling daemon, on 127.0.0.1 TCP
    7720: the Phone app's buttons as commands. telephonyd must be
    running (an ePhone starts it; on a Terminal with a modem run
    telephonyd &) and registered with the carrier to make calls.

    Where a command takes an optional call id, it means that call;
    without one, the ringing call, else the active one, else the
    first. Ids are the numbers tel calls shows.

COMMANDS
    status
        The default. Registered or not (VoLTE when it is), the phone's
        number, the network's domain, new voicemails, missed calls,
        unread texts, and the calls going on.

    dial <number> [--hide]
        Call a number: +12145550142, 214-555-0142, a local 555-0142,
        or a short code (611 support, *86 voicemail). --hide (or *67
        before the number) withholds your number for this call.

    answer, hangup, decline
        Answer a ringing call, end a call, or turn a ringing call
        away.

    hold on|off, mute on|off
        Put a call on hold or take it back; mute your microphone.

    swap, merge
        With two calls: swap which one is on hold, or merge both into
        a three-way call.

    speaker on|off
        Loudspeaker, or the earpiece.

    dtmf <digits>
        Keypad tones into the call (0-9, * and #), e.g. for the
        voicemail menu or the 611 support line.

    play <wav>, record <seconds> <wav>
        Send a WAV file to the far end instead of your microphone, or
        record what the far end says for that many seconds (1 to 300).
        The file must be under /home/ or /tmp/.

    calls
        The live calls: id, state, to or from, the time, HD, muted,
        3-way.

    log [n]
        The last n calls (default 20): in, out or missed, the number,
        how long and why it ended.

    voicemail
        The messages in your voicemail box, as visual voicemail lists
        them: vm id from time length new|heard.

    forward <number>, forward off
        Forward all your calls to a number (dials *72<number>), or stop
        (*73).

    watch
        Print calls as they happen (incoming, call states, keypad
        tones, voicemail, registration). Ctrl+C stops.

EXAMPLES
      tel
      tel dial 611
      tel dtmf 1
      tel hangup
      tel dial 214-555-0143 --hide
      tel answer
      tel record 10 /home/call.wav
      tel forward +12145550199

NOTES
    "telephonyd is not running": start it (telephonyd &) after celld.
    "no call": there is no call for the command to act on.
