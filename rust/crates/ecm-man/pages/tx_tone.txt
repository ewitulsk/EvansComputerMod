NAME
    tx_tone - transmit a test carrier or FM tone

SYNOPSIS
    tx_tone <freq> [--offset HZ] [--fm AUDIO_HZ] [--seconds S]
    [--power DBM] [--sdr NAME] [--rate SPS] [--iq FILE]

DESCRIPTION
    Keys the SDR's transmitter for a few seconds with a plain carrier
    (an unmodulated signal), or with --fm an FM signal carrying an
    audio tone. Use it to check that another station or a Handheld
    Radio hears you, to find your signal on a waterfall, or to test an
    antenna and feedline. Needs a Standard or Advanced SDR (a Basic SDR
    is receive-only).

    Prints the frequency and length, then "tx_tone: done" once the
    transmitter is off again.

OPTIONS
    --offset HZ
        Put the carrier HZ above (or, with a minus sign, below) freq.
        Must stay inside half the sample rate. Default 0.

    --fm AUDIO_HZ
        Send narrowband FM (5 kHz deviation) with a tone of AUDIO_HZ
        instead of a bare carrier. Needs --rate 16000 or more.

    --seconds S
        How long to transmit, up to 3600. Default 5.

    --power DBM
        Transmit power in dBm (0 = 1 mW, 30 = 1 W). Default: the SDR's
        maximum, 37 dBm (5 W).

    --sdr NAME
        Which SDR (empty = the first, 0, a side, or a /dev/sdr path).

    --rate SPS
        Sample rate. Default 48000.

    --iq FILE
        Write the would-be transmission to FILE (cf32 IQ plus a
        .sigmf-meta file) instead of transmitting.

EXAMPLES
      tx_tone 146.52M
      tx_tone 7.1M --fm 1000 --seconds 20 --power 30
      tx_tone 146.52M --offset 5k --power 0
